Delete your Expense Manager account

Expense Manager - Budget App is published by TechHigh. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time.

Option 1 – in the app (instant)

  1. Open Expense Manager and sign in.
  2. On the Expenses screen, tap the menu (⋮) in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm with Delete.

Option 2 – by email (if you no longer have the app)

Email support@techhigh.co.uk from the email address you used to sign up, with the subject "Delete my Expense Manager account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Delete individual expenses (keep your account)

To remove specific data without deleting your account, open Expense Manager and either long-press an expense, or tap it and choose Delete. Deleted expenses are removed from our database immediately. You can also delete your own categories under Menu (⋮) → Categories.

What gets deleted when you delete your account

  • Your account (name, email address and sign-in).
  • All expenses, categories, notes and your monthly budget.
  • Your notification token.

Nothing is kept after deletion, except where the law requires us to keep it.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is Chess Arena free?

Yes. It is free to play, with ads between games. There are no purchases.

Do I need an account?

Only to play online. Games against the computer, two players on one phone and puzzles work as a guest, even without internet.

How is my rating worked out?

With the standard Elo system: beating a stronger player earns more points, and losing to a weaker one costs more.

What happens if someone leaves a game?

If a player loses connection for a minute, or does not make their first move within 30 seconds, they lose the game.

Who will I play with Random opponent?

Another member who is online. If nobody is available, a computer-controlled club player takes the seat so you never wait; these games do not change your rating.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account, or follow the steps on the delete account page.

Privacy Policy

QR & Barcode Scanner. Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what the app does with your information. The app is published by TechHigh. You can contact us at support@techhigh.co.uk.

Codes you scan or create

Scanning happens on your device. The codes you scan or create, and your history, are stored only on your device. We do not receive them and have no servers that hold them. You can delete individual items, clear your whole history, or turn history off in Settings. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Camera

The camera is used only to read codes while the Scan screen is open. Camera images are processed on your device and are not recorded or uploaded.

Photos

If you choose to scan from your gallery or add a logo, the app reads only the picture you select, on your device. It has no access to your other photos.

Your logo

A logo you add in the QR Designer is read from the picture you select and used only on your device to draw your code. It is not uploaded, and is not kept after you leave the designer.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device information, and how you interact with ads, in order to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Scanned content is not sent to advertisers. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area or another region where consent is required, the app asks for your choice before showing personalised ads. You can change it at any time under Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Google services

Google ML Kit reads the codes on your device. It may send Google limited technical data, such as device and app information and performance metrics, and does not send the images or the code contents. Google Play is used to check for and install app updates.

When you act on a code

If you open a link, search the web, or send content to another app, that website or app handles your information under its own policy. Only open links you trust.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version at this address, with a new date.

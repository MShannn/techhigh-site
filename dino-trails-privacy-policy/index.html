Privacy Policy

Dino Trails

Last updated: 22 November 2026. This policy explains what happens to information when you play Dino Trails ("the game"), published by TechHigh (developer name Best4Use on the app stores). You can contact us at support@techhigh.co.uk.

The short version

The game collects nothing. There are no adverts, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no chat. You do not need to give a name, email, photo, contact or location, and the game does not ask for any device permissions. Your progress stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Solved levels, stars, coins, dinosaur friends, your park, your settings and whether you have unlocked the full game are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Removing the game or clearing its data deletes them.

Advertising and tracking

There is none. The game contains no advertising software, no analytics software and no third-party tracking code. It does not use the advertising ID.

The optional purchase

The game offers one optional, one-time purchase that unlocks the full game. It is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment details. The game only receives confirmation that the purchase was made so it can unlock the levels. The purchase is protected by a question for grown-ups, and "Restore purchase" brings it back on a new device that uses the same store account.

Children

Dino Trails is suitable for all ages. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. Links that lead out of the game and the purchase are placed behind a question for grown-ups.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. We hold no personal data about you. For data held by Apple or Google about a purchase, use the tools those companies provide.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with an updated date, in the game and on the game's store page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@techhigh.co.uk

Dino Trails

Dino Trails is a calm one-finger puzzle: tap the road pieces to turn them and lead the baby dinosaur home to its mum. 320 levels, no timers, no adverts.

Free to try on iPhone, iPad and Android. One optional purchase unlocks the full game. No adverts, no tracking, works without internet.

Common questions

How do I play? Tap a road piece to turn it. Turn the pieces until the road joins each baby dinosaur to its own mum (the same colour). The baby then walks home. The ? button on the level screen shows it with moving pictures.

I bought the full game but the chapters are locked. Open Settings and tap Restore purchase. Make sure the device uses the same Apple ID or Google account that made the purchase.

Is it a subscription? No. The full game is one single payment and it is yours forever. There are no ads and nothing else to buy.

How do I get more hints? You earn a free hint for every four new levels you solve and for every Daily Trail. A hint can also be bought with 30 coins, which you earn by playing.

Some pieces do not turn. Trees, stones, bridges and crossings stay where they are. Only the pieces on a raised plate turn.

How do I delete my data? The game keeps your progress only on your device and collects nothing. Removing the game deletes it. Settings also has Reset all progress.

Support

We answer every email, usually within one working day (UK time).

Contact us

  • Help with the app: support@techhigh.co.uk
  • Privacy and your data: privacy@techhigh.co.uk
  • Security issues: security@techhigh.co.uk (see the disclosure policy)

Please tell us your device, its system version and the Keyvane version (shown at the bottom of Settings). Never send us your master password, Secret Key, Emergency Kit or vault file. We never need them and we will never ask for them.

Open your vault on a new device

  1. Install Keyvane and choose I already have a vault.
  2. Pick the storage you used (iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, WebDAV, S3, your own Firebase, a folder or another device over Wi-Fi) and sign in to it.
  3. Enter your master password. On iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to the same Apple account, your Secret Key comes from iCloud Keychain. Elsewhere, scan the QR code on your Emergency Kit or type the Secret Key.

Forgotten master password

Check your Emergency Kit. If you have lost both your master password and the kit, nobody can open the vault, including TechHigh. That is what end-to-end encryption means. If you still have access on another device, export a backup from there, then create a new vault.

Subscriptions, cancelling and refunds

  • iPhone, iPad and Mac: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Keyvane. Refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Android: Google Play › profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Keyvane. Refunds: Google Play refund help.
  • Moved to a new phone? Open the plans screen from Settings and tap Restore purchases.
  • Cancel at least 24 hours before a trial or period ends to avoid the next charge. Without a plan your vault stays readable and exportable.

Autofill is not showing

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings › General › AutoFill & Passwords › turn on Keyvane.
  • Mac: System Settings › General › AutoFill & Passwords › turn on Keyvane.
  • Android: Settings › Passwords, passkeys & accounts (or System › Languages & input › Autofill service) › choose Keyvane.
  • Open Keyvane once after changing logins so the autofill index updates.

Deleting your data

Keyvane has no accounts, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To remove everything, delete the app and delete the .kvault file from the storage you chose (for Google Drive, remove Keyvane under Google Account › Security › Third-party apps, which also clears its hidden folder). Read more in the Privacy Policy.

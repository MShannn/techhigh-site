The short version

  • Your vault is encrypted on your device before it is saved anywhere. TechHigh never receives your vault contents, your master password or your Secret Key.
  • TechHigh runs no server that receives your vault, and there are no Keyvane user accounts. You do not sign up with us.
  • You choose where the encrypted vault is stored: this device only, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, another of your devices over local Wi-Fi, or (advanced) WebDAV, an S3-compatible bucket, your own Firebase project or a synced folder. That provider only ever holds an encrypted file.
  • When you start a free trial or subscribe, Apple or Google take payment, and RevenueCat processes the purchase receipt and subscription status so the app can unlock your plan. We never see your card details.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting, and no cookies on this website.

2. What we never receive

Keyvane is designed so that the following never reach TechHigh in any form. We do not collect them, we cannot access them, and we cannot recover them for you:

  • your master password;
  • your Secret Key;
  • your vault key;
  • the contents of your vault: client names, item titles, usernames, passwords, passkeys, URLs, notes, tags, attachments, 2FA secrets and codes, reminders and history;
  • the encrypted vault file itself, which goes only to the storage you choose.

We do not operate user accounts, so we hold no email address, name or profile for you unless you email us (section 13). How the encryption works is described on the Security page.

3. What the app keeps on your device

The app keeps the following locally, on the device you install it on. None of it is sent to TechHigh.

  • Your encrypted vault file and a local backup copy of it.
  • Your Secret Key, in the device's secure keychain or equivalent (Apple Keychain, Android Keystore, or Windows Credential Manager).
  • If you enable biometric unlock, the vault key, in the device keychain behind the biometric prompt.
  • If you turn on autofill, a separate encrypted autofill index (section 6).
  • Sign-in tokens for any storage provider you connect (for example Google Drive or Dropbox), issued by that provider, or the connection details you enter for advanced storage.
  • Settings and preferences, such as auto-lock time and theme, and scheduled reminder notifications (section 9).

Deleting the app removes these, except where your operating system keeps keychain entries or a backup of app data; see your platform's documentation.

4. Your storage provider

You choose where your encrypted vault is kept:

  • This device only — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • iCloud Drive (Apple) — the file is saved in Keyvane's iCloud container in your Apple account.
  • Google Drive — Keyvane asks only for the drive.appdata permission, which gives it a hidden app-data folder. Keyvane cannot see or open any of your other Drive files.
  • Dropbox — Keyvane uses an app folder (Apps/Keyvane) and cannot see the rest of your Dropbox.
  • OneDrive (Microsoft) — Keyvane asks only for Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder (its own Apps/Keyvane folder), offline_access (to stay signed in) and User.Read (your name and email, shown in the app so you know which account is connected). It cannot see the rest of your OneDrive.
  • Device to device — the encrypted file is sent directly between your own devices over your local Wi-Fi network after QR pairing. It does not pass through the internet or through us.
  • Advanced — a WebDAV server, an S3-compatible bucket, your own Firebase project, or a folder on a desktop computer that you sync with another tool.

In every case the vault is encrypted on your device before upload, and the file goes directly from your device to the provider using your own account with them. TechHigh is not involved in the transfer and does not receive a copy. The provider can see the encrypted file, its name and size, when it changed, and whatever metadata their service records about any file (such as your IP address). They cannot read its contents.

The provider acts under your own agreement with them, and their own privacy policy applies to that file and to your account: for example Apple, Google and Dropbox. For advanced storage, the operator of that server, bucket or project is responsible for it.

5. Secret Key and iCloud Keychain

Unlocking a vault needs your master password and your 160-bit Secret Key. On Apple devices (iPhone, iPad, Mac) the app also saves the Secret Key in your iCloud Keychain, which Apple end-to-end encrypts, so that a new Apple device only needs your master password. This is handled by Apple under your Apple account and Apple's privacy policy; TechHigh cannot access it. If iCloud Keychain is turned off in your Apple account settings, the key is kept only on the device and on your Emergency Kit.

On Android and Windows the Secret Key is entered from your Emergency Kit (by scanning its QR code or typing it) when you set up a new device. The Secret Key is never sent to TechHigh or to your storage provider. The Security page explains the trade-off this makes.

6. Autofill

If you turn on autofill, Keyvane registers as an autofill provider with your operating system: the Android Autofill service and Credential Manager (to fill and save passwords, and passkeys on Android 14 and later), or the iOS and macOS AutoFill credential provider (to fill logins, and on iOS 26.2 and later to save new ones). To do this the app keeps a separate, encrypted autofill index on the device, unlocked with your biometrics or device passcode. A password you ask to save while Keyvane is locked is kept encrypted on the device, in an area only Keyvane can read, until you next open Keyvane, which then adds it to your vault; it is deleted after 7 days if you don't. The index stays on the device and is not sent to TechHigh. When you fill a login, the selected username and password are passed to the app or website you are signing in to, through your operating system.

7. Sharing a login

You can choose to share a single login's username, password and website as plain text through your device's share sheet, or by copying it to the clipboard, after confirming with biometrics. Keyvane does not send it anywhere itself. Once shared, what you sent is handled by the app or person you shared it with (for example a messaging or email app), under their terms and privacy policy, and is no longer protected by Keyvane's encryption.

8. Breach check

The optional breach check uses the Have I Been Pwned Pwned Passwords service and its k-anonymity model. For each password the app computes a SHA-1 hash on your device and sends only the first 5 characters of that hash (with padding requested) to api.pwnedpasswords.com. The service returns a list of matching hash endings and the comparison happens on your device. Your passwords, their full hashes and your items never leave the device. As with any web request, the service sees your IP address. The check runs only when you start it.

9. Reminders and notifications

Expiry reminders (a Pro feature) are scheduled as local notifications on your device. No push-notification service is used and nothing about your reminders is sent to us.

10. No analytics, ads or crash reporting

The Keyvane apps and extension contain no analytics, telemetry, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting libraries. If the app crashes, nothing is sent to us. Your operating system or app store may offer to send crash reports to the platform vendor under their own policies; this is controlled by your device settings, not by Keyvane. Apple and Google also give us aggregate statistics about downloads, ratings and sales that do not identify you.

11. Purchases (Apple, Google, RevenueCat)

Keyvane plans (Basic, Pro, Pro Yearly and Pro Lifetime, including the 3-day free trials) are bought only inside the app through Apple App Store in-app purchase or Google Play Billing, using your store account (which may use Apple Pay or Google Pay). Apple or Google take your payment under their own terms and privacy policies. TechHigh never sees your card or bank details.

We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., revenuecat.com) as our processor to check purchases and unlock your plan. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated by the app, the purchase and receipt data from Apple or Google (such as product, transaction identifiers, purchase and renewal dates, price, currency and store country), and your subscription and trial status (for example whether a trial is active, when it ends, and whether a subscription is active, cancelled or expired), and basic technical data such as app version, platform and IP address. It does not receive your vault data, master password, Secret Key, name or email address. We can see that subscription information in RevenueCat's dashboard, linked only to the anonymous ID.

Subscriptions are managed, cancelled and refunded in your App Store or Google Play account settings, not by us.

12. This website and cookies

This website (techhigh.co.uk/keyvane) is a static site. It sets no cookies, uses no similar storage, runs no analytics and contains no third-party scripts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google receives the request for the font files together with your IP address and browser details; see Google Fonts privacy.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., privacy statement), our hosting provider, which processes connection data such as your IP address to deliver pages and protect against abuse. We do not access visitor-level logs.

The Keyvane web app, where you use it, keeps your encrypted vault and settings in your browser's local storage so that it can work offline; that data stays in your browser and is not a tracking mechanism.

13. Support email

If you email us, we receive your email address, name (if you include it) and whatever you write, and keep the correspondence so we can answer you and follow up. Please do not send us passwords, Secret Keys or vault contents; we never need them and will ask you to delete the message if you do.

15. Lawful bases

Under Article 6 of the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)): verifying your trial or purchase through RevenueCat and unlocking your plan.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): delivering and protecting this website, answering support emails, and understanding aggregate store statistics. We have balanced these against your interests and use the minimum data needed.
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)): keeping sales records required by tax and accounting law.

Features that send data to a third party at your request, such as connecting a storage provider, running a breach check or sharing a login, happen because you ask for them, and you can stop using them at any time.

16. Retention

  • Vault data: we hold none. Your vault stays on your devices and in the storage you chose for as long as you keep it there.
  • Purchase records (in RevenueCat and our accounts): for as long as your subscription is active and then for 6 years, as required by UK tax law.
  • Support correspondence: 2 years after the last message.
  • Website connection data: kept by GitHub under its own retention periods; we do not keep visitor logs.

17. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • rectify inaccurate data;
  • erase your data ("right to be forgotten");
  • restrict or object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • data portability;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Because your vault is encrypted with keys we do not have and is never sent to us, we cannot provide, correct or delete its contents for you — but you can, at any time, from inside the app: export it in several formats, change anything, or delete it, and you can delete the encrypted file from your storage provider directly. For purchase data, include your anonymous app user ID or your store receipt so we can find the record. To exercise any right, email privacy@techhigh.co.uk. We will respond within one month.

18. International transfers

RevenueCat is based in the United States, and Apple, Google and GitHub may process data outside the UK and the European Economic Area. Where personal data we are responsible for is transferred to a country without an adequacy decision, we rely on appropriate safeguards: the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified, or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. Storage providers you choose yourself (section 4) process data wherever your account with them is located, under your agreement with them.

19. Children

Keyvane is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age required in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Because there are no Keyvane accounts, we do not collect age information. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

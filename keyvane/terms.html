4. Zero knowledge: we cannot recover your vault

Please read this section carefully. Keyvane is built so that only you can open your vault. Your master password and Secret Key never leave your devices in a form we can read. This means:

  • We cannot reset your master password.
  • We cannot recover your Secret Key.
  • We cannot open, read, repair or hand over the contents of your vault, to you or to anyone else, including under a court order — we simply do not have the keys.
  • If you lose both your master password and your Emergency Kit, your vault is permanently unrecoverable. Nobody, including TechHigh, can help.

The app asks you to print or save an Emergency Kit when you sign up and re-checks your master password periodically. You accept that keeping that kit safe is your responsibility, and that we are not liable for loss of access caused by a forgotten password or a lost Secret Key.

Organisation accounts on the Business plan may enable account recovery, which lets a nominated administrator restore a member's access through an additional encrypted key. Where enabled, recovery is performed by your organisation, not by TechHigh.

Delete your Chess Arena account

Chess Arena is published by TechHigh. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time.

Option 1 – in the app (instant)

  1. Open Chess Arena and sign in.
  2. Tap the gear (Settings) at the top right of the home screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account, then confirm with Delete. If you signed in a while ago, the app asks you to sign in again first.

Option 2 – by email (if you no longer have the app)

Email support@techhigh.co.uk from the email address you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Chess Arena account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Remove some data and keep your account

In the app you can remove your profile photo (Settings > Edit profile > Change photo > Remove photo), change your name, country and city, and remove friends (Friends > ⋮ > Remove friend).

What gets deleted when you delete your account

  • Your sign-in account, display name, avatar or photo, country, city and player ID.
  • Your rating, record, puzzle points and game history.
  • Your friends list, friend requests and any open challenges.

Moves and chat messages inside games you played with others may remain in that game's record for the other player. Nothing else is kept, except where the law requires us to keep it.

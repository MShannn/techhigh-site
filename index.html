Car rental & dealer platformiOS · Android · Web

Rentacar

One platform for car rental companies and car dealers. Customers find a car nearby, book it or buy it on installments, while owners run branches, fleet, agreements and payments from the dashboard and the app.

  • Bookings with digital rental agreements
  • Car sales with installment plans
  • Service history and garage for every car
  • English, Urdu and Arabic, right to left
Request a demoSee all features

Everything in Rentacar

Branches & fleet

Plan-limited branches, each with its own staff, cars and bookings. Full specs, photos and daily, weekly or monthly pricing, self-drive or with driver.

Nearby discovery

Customers search by city, radius, make and filters, save favourites and see an availability calendar for every car.

Bookings & agreements

Request, accept, reservation payment, then a rental agreement with CNIC, deposit or guarantor and fuel policy. Check-out and check-in with odometer, fuel and damage photos.

Payments

Reservation, rent and installment payments through one layer: cash, bank transfer, Stripe and local wallets such as JazzCash and Easypaisa.

Car sales & installments

Sale listings with buyer inquiries, dealer installment plans with down payment and markup, automatic schedules, late fees and due reminders.

Service & garage

Service records and "service due" reminders for fleet cars and for customers' own cars, with mechanic-verified history.

Notifications

In-app, push and WhatsApp messages for every booking step, sent in each user's language.

Plans & feature switches

Subscription plans built from features and limits. The platform owner can switch any module on or off per company.

Remote app control

Maintenance mode, minimum app version, banners and enabled modules per platform, served when the app starts.

Roles for everyone

Super admin, company owner, branch manager, staff, customer and mechanic, each seeing only what they should.

Multilingual

English, Urdu and Arabic with full right-to-left layout across the API, dashboard and app.

Owner dashboard

A web dashboard for companies to manage cars, bookings, sales, installments and staff in one place.

Staff & catering managementWeb · iOS · Android

RotaDesk

Rota, clock-in, leave and catering orders in one place for restaurants and shops. Managers plan the week on the web dashboard; staff check shifts, clock in and request leave from the app.

  • Build and publish the rota in minutes
  • Clock-in only at the shop, checked on the server
  • Catering orders, kitchen board and prep sheet
  • Available in 21 languages
Request a demoSee all features

Everything in RotaDesk

Weekly rota

Add shifts, copy last week (skipping anyone on leave) and publish. Drafts stay hidden from staff, and changes to published shifts notify the person automatically.

Clock-in at work only

One-tap clock in, clock out and breaks. Each location has a radius set on a map, and the check runs on the server so it can't be bypassed.

Who's in today

Managers see the whole team at a glance: who is on the rota, who has clocked in and who is late.

Leave

Holiday and sick requests come straight to the manager for one-tap approval, with days off and upcoming leave in one list.

Hours & payroll

Timesheets with paid hours and breaks, corrections that need a reason and are written to an audit log, and payroll export.

Roles & permissions

Choose exactly what each person can manage: rota, leave, hours, staff, orders, prices, menu or locations. Enforced in the database.

Catering orders

Menu, dishes and catering packages, order booking with numbering from #1001, confirm and assign to staff, with a calendar view.

Kitchen board

A live board of new, in-kitchen and ready orders so the kitchen always knows what is next.

Prep sheet

Adds up every dish, unit and spice level for the day across all orders, ready to print.

Staff & documents

Staff profiles, job titles and rates, with documents kept in private storage behind links that expire after two minutes.

Multi-business

Many businesses and locations, each with its own branding, plus a super-admin console to approve, pause and manage them.

21 languages

English, Urdu, Arabic, Polish, Spanish, Portuguese, Hindi and more, chosen per business or per phone.

End-to-end encrypted vaultAndroid · iOS · Mac · Windows · Linux · Web

Keyvane

Your keys. Your cloud. Nobody else. A credential vault for developers, freelancers and agencies: every client login, API key and 2FA code is encrypted on the device and stored wherever you choose.

  • Zero knowledge: we only ever see encrypted bytes
  • Store it in your own Drive, iCloud, Dropbox or server
  • Works offline, autofills everywhere
  • Organised by client, project and environment
Join the betaSee all features

Everything in Keyvane

Zero-knowledge encryption

Your master password and Secret Key never leave your devices. Storage providers, and TechHigh, only ever receive encrypted bytes.

Your own storage

Keep the vault in Google Drive, iCloud, Dropbox, OneDrive, WebDAV, S3, your own server, device-to-device sync or just the device. Move it any time.

Local-first

The vault opens, edits and autofills offline. The cloud is only a mirror, and changes merge safely between devices.

Built for client work

Clients, projects and environments, account ownership, records of who holds each 2FA, expiry reminders and a clean handover when a job ends.

Built-in 2FA codes

A TOTP authenticator inside the vault, so the password and its one-time code live together.

Autofill everywhere

Native autofill on Android and iOS, a browser extension for Chrome and Firefox, and the kv command-line tool.

Ready-made item types

Logins, Google accounts, Apple Developer, Google Play Console, Firebase projects, API keys, GitHub, servers and SSH.

Safe sharing

Choose exactly which fields to send to a client or colleague, or share encrypted with your team through Keyvane Cloud organisations.

Password generator

Strong passwords and passphrases with a live strength meter on every entry.

Import & export

An open, documented vault format. Import from other managers and export whenever you like: no lock-in.

Every device

One Flutter app for Android, iOS, macOS, Windows, Linux and the web, with the same vault everywhere.

Teams

Keyvane Cloud adds organisations and shared vaults for agencies, still end-to-end encrypted.

Installment phone financingIn development

PhoneLock

For shops that sell Android phones on installment. If a payment is missed, the shop can lock the phone remotely until it is paid, with no root needed. One platform runs many shops, each under its own brand.

  • Remote lock and unlock from the shop's phone
  • A friendly lock screen with one-tap payment
  • JazzCash and Easypaisa built in
  • English and Urdu, light and dark themes
Register interestSee all features

Everything in PhoneLock

Device Owner app

A native Android app enrolled with a QR code as the device owner, using Android's own device policy controls. No rooting.

Remote & offline locking

Lock or unlock a phone instantly from the dashboard, with locking that still works when the phone is offline.

Friendly lock screen

Shows the shop's brand, the amount due and days overdue, with Pay now, "I've already paid", Call shop and Emergency always available.

Local payments

Customers pay through JazzCash or Easypaisa straight from the lock screen.

Payment claims

When a customer taps "I've already paid", the shop sees a claim to check and confirm, then unlocks in one tap.

Dealer dashboard

Collected today against the monthly target, active and overdue devices, and a "needs attention" list with one-tap lock and unlock.

Lock alerts

Phones about to lock are flagged hours ahead, so staff can call the customer first.

Customers & stock

Enrol devices, record payments, and keep customers, installment balances and phone stock in one place.

Multi-shop SaaS

One platform, many shops. A super-admin console handles shops, billing and plans; each shop only sees its own data.

White-label

Every shop gets its own name, logo and colours on the lock screen and dashboard.

English & Urdu

Full Urdu, right to left, for customers and shop staff.

Light & dark themes

Clean Light and Midnight themes that users can switch between.

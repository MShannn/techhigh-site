DeenDost Kids Privacy Policy

Learn Islam the fun way — stories, duas, surahs and how to pray

Last updated: 19 October 2026

DeenDost Kids ("the app", "we", "us") is published on Google Play and the Apple App Store by Best4Use, the maker of DeenDost. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. DeenDost Kids is made for children and their families. It has no account, no login, no adverts and no in-app purchases.

1. Information we use

InformationWhyWhere it is stored
Learning progress (stars from quizzes)To show your child's stars and progress.On the phone only.
App settings (light or dark theme)To make the app look the way you set it.On the phone only.

We do not collect contacts, messages, call logs, health data, financial information or advertising identifiers. DeenDost Kids contains no advertising and does not sell or share your data.

DeenDost Kids does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts or any personal details, and does not use advertising identifiers or analytics. Settings and the link to other apps are behind a grown-ups check.

2. Third-party services

  • Recitation audio and kids' videos served from content delivery networks and the DeenDost website
  • Google Firebase (app settings and database) — privacy

These services receive only what they need to answer a request, plus the technical data any internet request includes, such as your IP address.

3. How long we keep data

Data stored on your phone stays until you clear it or uninstall the app. Any account data is kept while your account exists and removed when you delete it.

4. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

5. Children

This app is designed for children. We do not collect personal information from children or anyone else. Stories, videos and recitations are downloaded from our servers like any web request, which includes the phone's IP address; we do not use it to identify anyone.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.

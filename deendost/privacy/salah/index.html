Salah Privacy Policy

Prayer times, Azan alarms, Qibla and prayer tracker

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Salah ("the app", "we", "us") is published on Google Play and the Apple App Store by Best4Use, the maker of DeenDost. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. Salah works without an account. Your prayer times, alarms and prayer record stay on your phone.

1. Information we use

InformationWhyWhere it is stored
Location (precise or approximate)To calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction for where you are.On your phone only. If you search for a town by name, the name is sent to the phone's geocoding service. We do not store your location.
Prayer tracker recordTo show which prayers you prayed, missed or made up, and your charts.On your phone only.
App settings: method, alarms, soundTo make the app work the way you set it.On your phone only.

We do not collect contacts, messages, call logs, health data, financial information or advertising identifiers. Salah contains no advertising and does not sell or share your data.

Salah uses notification and alarm permissions only to ring at prayer times, and asks Android not to restrict it for battery saving so alarms are on time. You can change both in your phone settings.

2. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (app settings and database) — privacy
  • The phone's built-in geocoding service (to turn a searched town name into coordinates)

These services receive only what they need to answer a request, plus the technical data any internet request includes, such as your IP address.

3. How long we keep data

Data stored on your phone stays until you clear it or uninstall the app. Any account data is kept while your account exists and removed when you delete it.

4. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

5. Children

Salah is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.

Halal Scanner Privacy Policy

Scan a product to see if it is halal, and find halal food near you

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Halal Scanner ("the app", "we", "us") is published on Google Play and the Apple App Store by Best4Use, the maker of DeenDost. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. Halal Scanner works without an account. Logging in is optional and only needed to report products.

1. Information we use

InformationWhyWhere it is stored
Camera / photosTo scan barcodes and read ingredient labels.Processed on your phone. Photos are not uploaded or stored by us. Barcodes are looked up on Open Food Facts and the DeenDost product database.
Location (precise or approximate)To find halal restaurants and butchers near you.On your phone. When you look up nearby places, approximate coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap services to return results. We do not store your location.
Recent scans and favourite brandsSo you can see products you checked again.On your phone only. You can clear them in the app.
Account details (only if you log in with Google or Apple): name, email, user IDTo let you report products and keep a count of your reports.Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore).
Product reports you submitTo improve halal information for everyone.Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore).

We do not collect contacts, messages, call logs, health data, financial information or advertising identifiers. Halal Scanner contains no advertising and does not sell or share your data.

You can delete your account in the app (More → your profile → Delete account) or at deendost-uk.web.app/delete-account.

2. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (app settings and database) — privacy
  • Open Food Facts (product information) — privacy
  • OpenStreetMap (maps and nearby places) — privacy
  • Google ML Kit (text recognition runs on the phone)

These services receive only what they need to answer a request, plus the technical data any internet request includes, such as your IP address.

3. How long we keep data

Data stored on your phone stays until you clear it or uninstall the app. Any account data is kept while your account exists and removed when you delete it.

4. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

5. Children

Halal Scanner is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Logging in is optional.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.

Privacy Policy

Dino Puzzle Pals

Last updated: 22 November 2026. This policy explains what happens to information when your child plays Dino Puzzle Pals ("the game"), published by TechHigh (developer name Best4Use on the app stores). You can contact us at support@techhigh.co.uk.

The short version

The game collects nothing. There are no adverts, no accounts, no chat, no analytics and no tracking. It works without an internet connection. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

What stays on your device

The stars your child has earned, the dinosaurs they have found, the sound settings, and whether the full game has been unlocked are saved only on the device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Removing the game or clearing its data deletes them.

Adverts and tracking

There are none. The game contains no advertising software and no analytics software, and it does not use any advertising identifier.

The voice

Dinosaur names are spoken by the voice already built into your phone or tablet. Nothing is recorded and the game never uses the microphone.

The one purchase

The game is free to download with six puzzles. A single optional purchase unlocks every puzzle forever. It sits behind a question for grown-ups and is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation so that it can unlock the puzzles. On a new device, use "Restore purchase" in the parents' area to get it back at no cost.

Children

The game is made for young children and is designed so that no personal information is collected from them. There are no links out of the game and no social features.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you or your child, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. For data held by Apple or Google about a purchase, use the tools those companies provide.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown here with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Write to support@techhigh.co.uk

Privacy Policy

Crackle Yard (formerly Smash Yard). Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what the game does with your information. The game is published by TechHigh. You can contact us at support@techhigh.co.uk.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing. The game has no accounts and we run no servers. Your progress, stars, boosters and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device information, diagnostics, and how you interact with the game and with ads, in order to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area, Switzerland or another region where consent is required, the game asks for your choice before showing personalised ads. You can change it at any time under Shop > Privacy choices in the game. On iPhone and iPad, tracking across apps also needs your permission through the system prompt. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment details. The game only receives confirmation of what was bought, so that it can unlock it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

We hold no personal data about you ourselves, so there is nothing for us to delete. Requests about advertising data should be made to Google using the link above. You can delete all game data by uninstalling the game.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version at this address, with a new date.

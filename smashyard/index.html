How it works

Playing in four steps

  1. Swipe to match

    Swap two neighbours to line up three or more of the same thing.

  2. Build power-ups

    Bigger matches make Jackhammers, Dynamite and the Wrecking Ball.

  3. Hit the goal

    Finish the level goal before you run out of moves.

  4. Earn stars

    Leftover moves turn into a power-up frenzy and a higher score.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is Crackle Yard free?

Yes. It is free to play, with ads and optional in-app purchases for booster packs and for removing the ads between levels.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. You can play every level offline. Ads, the free video rewards and purchases need a connection.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, and your progress is stored on your phone.

When do ads appear?

Only between levels, never during one, and not in your first few levels. Video rewards are always your choice.

Is it on iPhone?

The Android version is in testing on Google Play. The iPhone and iPad version is coming soon.

Chess Arena – Privacy Policy

App: Chess Arena (package com.techhig.chess_arena)
Publisher: TechHigh
Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information Chess Arena collects, how it is used and the choices you have. You can play against the computer, play two players on one phone and solve puzzles as a guest without an account; nothing from those games leaves your phone.

1. Information we collect

Only if you sign in to play online:

  • Sign-in: your email address and, for Google sign-in, your Google account name and ID. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us. Your public profile stores only a one-way scrambled form (hash) of your email, so other players can find you only if they already know your exact address.
  • Your profile: display name, chosen avatar or the profile photo you upload (kept as a small image), country, optional city and your player ID. These are visible to other players.
  • Games and ranking: your rating, wins, losses and draws, puzzle points, the moves of your online games and your game history.
  • Social: your friends list, friend requests, challenges and the chat messages you send in games.
  • Activity: the time you were last active, used to show who is online and to end games a player has left.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or payment details. Chess Arena has no purchases.

2. How we use your information

  • To run online games, challenges, rooms, spectating, chat, friends and the city, country and world leaderboards.
  • To calculate ratings and keep your game history.
  • To show you in-app notices when someone challenges you or sends a friend request.

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Computer opponents

When no other player is available for a random game, Chess Arena may match you with a computer-controlled club player so that you never wait for a game. Games against club players do not change your rating or the leaderboards. Challenges you send to a friend or a named player are always with that person.

Advertising

Chess Arena shows ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device information and how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Your games, chats and profile are not sent to advertisers. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area, Switzerland or another region where consent is required, the app asks for your choice before showing personalised ads, and you can change it later under Settings > Privacy choices for ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

4. Where your data is stored

Online data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, hosted in London, UK), operated by Google LLC, and encrypted in transit. Security rules limit what each player can change. See Firebase privacy and security. App updates are delivered by Google Play.

5. Sharing

Your profile, rating, game history and the moves of online games are visible to other players, and anyone signed in can watch a live game. We do not share your data with anyone else, except Google as our hosting and advertising provider, or if required by law.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: Settings > Delete account.
  • Without the app: follow the steps at techhigh.co.uk/chess-arena-delete-account.

Deleting your account removes your profile, photo, rating, friends, friend requests, game history and sign-in. Moves and chat messages inside games you played with others may stay in that game's record for the other player.

7. Children

Chess Arena is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR or EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Contact us to exercise these rights. You may also complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.

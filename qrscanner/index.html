How it works

Up and running in four steps

  1. Point and scan

    Open the app and point the camera at any QR code or barcode.

  2. Act on it

    Open the link, join the Wi-Fi or save the contact in one tap.

  3. Create a code

    Make a code for a link, Wi-Fi, contact or text.

  4. Make it yours

    Pick a template, colours and your logo, then save or share.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Scanning runs on your phone and works offline.

Is my scan history uploaded?

No. History is stored on your phone only.

Can I add my logo to a QR code?

Yes. The QR Designer lets you add a logo, pick shapes and colours, and checks the code still scans.

Is it free?

Yes. It is free with light ads that never appear on launch or while you scan.

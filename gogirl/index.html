Features

Everything in GoGirl

Ride now

Riders see online drivers nearby, offer a fare, and drivers accept or counter-offer. Then arriving, started, completed, with cash to the driver.

Daily commute

Post your route, find matching drivers or riders for the daily commute, and pause it on days you are not going.

Verified members only

Every member uploads documents and is checked and approved by a person before joining.

SOS & live location

One tap sends an SOS with live location to guardians and nearby drivers, with emergency numbers built in.

Guardians & family share

Add guardians and share a live trip link with family so they can follow the ride.

Private chat

Chat with your driver or rider with quick replies and read receipts. Phone numbers are never shown.

Ratings & referrals

Weekly ratings with tags keep standards high, and referral codes reward members who invite friends.

Support & help centre

Raise tickets with attachments, read the help centre in English or Urdu, or reach the team on WhatsApp.

Maps & place search

Search places and pick routes on the map, with Google Maps or OpenStreetMap.

English & Urdu

Admin panel

Approvals, members, rides, tickets, safety, broadcasts and reports in one admin panel.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Who can join GoGirl?

Only women. Every rider and driver is checked and approved by our team before joining.

How are fares decided?

The rider offers a fare and drivers can accept it or make a counter-offer.

What happens if I feel unsafe?

Tap SOS to send your live location to your guardians and nearby drivers, with emergency numbers one tap away.

Is my phone number shared?

No. You chat inside the app, and phone numbers are never shown.
